## Technical Implementation

All themes include:
- **Full color palette**: Accent, secondary accent, headline colors
- **Light/dark mode support**: Separate backgrounds and headline colors for both modes
- **Background tints**: Auto-generated 3% lighter/darker variants
- **Typography settings**: Custom fonts where appropriate (serif, rounded, condensed)
- **Duotone support**: Automatic highlight and shadow color calculation

## Font Styles Used

- **System** (default): Most themes
- **Rounded**: Red Bull-BORA, AlUla Jayco, La Vie Claire, EF Education-EasyPost, Mexico
- **Condensed**: Visma | Lease a Bike, Team Sky
- **Serif**: France, Italy, United Kingdom
- **Monospaced**: Alpecin-Deceuninck

## Total Themes in Cyclismo

- **Built-in themes**: 26 (7 original + 19 new)
- **Custom themes**: User-created themes (unlimited)

All themes are now available in the Themes menu of the Cyclismo app!

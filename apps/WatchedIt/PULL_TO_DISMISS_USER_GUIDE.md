# Pull-to-Dismiss User Guide

## What is Pull-to-Dismiss?

Pull-to-Dismiss is a new gesture that makes it easier to close bottom sheets in WatchedIt. Instead of reaching for the close button or swiping down, you can simply scroll to the bottom and pull to dismiss.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Open a Bottom Sheet
Open any of these screens:
- Account menu
- Movie details
- Themes selector
- Theme builder
- Onboarding configuration sheets

### Step 2: Scroll to the Bottom
Scroll down through the content until you reach the very bottom.

### Step 3: Pull Past the Bottom
Once at the bottom, continue pulling down. A close button (×) will appear at the bottom center of the sheet.

### Step 4: Pull Further to Activate
Keep pulling down a bit more. You'll feel a haptic vibration, and the button will:
- Turn bright (yellow for Batman theme, green for Matrix, etc.)
- Grow slightly larger (4% bigger)
- Become fully visible

### Step 5: Release to Dismiss
Let go, and the sheet will close automatically!

### Canceling
If you change your mind, just scroll back up a little and the button will disappear. The sheet stays open.

## Tips

- **Theme Colors**: The active button color matches your selected theme
- **Haptic Feedback**: Feel a gentle vibration when the button activates (iPhone only)
- **Works with Swipe**: You can still swipe down from the top to close sheets normally
- **Short Content**: If the content doesn't scroll, this gesture won't activate

## Why Use Pull-to-Dismiss?

- **One-handed**: Keep your thumb in the scrolling position
- **Natural**: Continues the scrolling gesture you're already doing
- **Discoverable**: The button appears when you need it
- **Reversible**: Easy to cancel by scrolling back up

## Troubleshooting

**Q: The button doesn't appear**
- Make sure you've scrolled all the way to the bottom first
- The content must be scrollable (taller than the screen)

**Q: The button appears but stays gray**
- Pull down further! It needs a bit more pull to activate
- Look for the size increase and color change

**Q: I let go but the sheet didn't close**
- The button needs to be in the active state (bright + bigger)
- Try pulling a bit more next time before releasing

**Q: Can I disable this feature?**
- Not currently, but it won't interfere with normal usage
- You can still close sheets using the standard methods

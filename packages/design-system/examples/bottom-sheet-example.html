Bottom Sheet Content

Grab the handle or any sheet edge, then drag in any direction. Fling to dismiss, or release to snap back.

Notice how the backdrop blur and darkening effects change as you adjust the detent size:

  • Small detent: Light blur (2px) and minimal darkening (10% opacity)
  • Medium detent: Moderate blur (8px) and medium darkening (30% opacity)
  • Large detent: Strong blur (16px) and heavy darkening (50% opacity)
